## 🎮 About the Game

The Adventurers is a turn-based tactical RPG developed and published by Eternal Vigilance Entertainment. It entered Steam Early Access on November 11, 2024, and reached full release on August 31, 2026. Set in a high-fantasy world inspired by classic tabletop RPGs, it combines party management, open-world exploration, quests, inventory management, and strategic combat.

## 🔥 Project Overview

**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **The Adventurers**. This trainer enhances the party-management and tactical RPG systems in The Adventurers. It provides control over movement, character progression, currency, inventory supplies, and combat-related advantages while preserving the game's party-based structure, quests, random events, hex-grid battles, equipment, crafting, and exploration.

**Target Audience:** Suitable for players who want to experiment with party builds, test classes and abilities, reduce resource management, revisit quests more efficiently, or customize the difficulty of tactical battles in single-player sessions.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

The Adventurers uses overworld travel to connect towns, villages, wilderness areas, ruins, quest locations, and encounters. Movement-related options are best used after the party is selected and the world map is active. Disable them before loading a different save or entering a major quest location to reduce the chance of unintended movement or event behavior.

### Tools & Inventory

The game includes equipment, consumable items, gathered materials, shops, buying and selling, and crafting. Inventory options should be activated only after the inventory or shop interface has loaded. Spend or move one item first to confirm that the option affects the intended stack, then use it for broader crafting, equipment, or supply management.

### World & Environment

The open world contains quests, random events, traveling threats, towns, villages, forests, mountains, ruins, and other points of interest. Currency and movement options are useful for exploring freely and preparing the party, while quest-related options should be used carefully because quest objectives and event outcomes are tied to the current save state.

### Combat & Weapons

Battles take place on hexagonal tactical maps where positioning, terrain, class abilities, spells, skills, and party composition determine the outcome. Activate defensive or offensive options after combat begins and the party's turn order is visible. For a more controlled experience, use unlimited health or ability options without enabling instant defeat or frozen enemies.
